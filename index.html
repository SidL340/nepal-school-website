About Our School

Decades of educational leadership and service to Madhesh Province

Quality Education Since 2007 B.S.

Shree Nepal Secondary School, Bishrampur is a leading government community secondary school located in Brindaban Municipality-02, Rautahat, Nepal.

Affiliated with the National Examination Board (NEB), we provide comprehensive learning from Early Childhood Development (ECD) to Grade 12 Higher Secondary streams in Management, Education, and Humanities.

NEB Approved
Govt. Curriculum
+2 Streams
Mgmt, Edu, Hum

💡 Vision & Mission

Vision: To nurture disciplined, knowledgeable, and socially responsible global citizens with strong moral values.

Mission: Deliver accessible, high-quality, and ICT-enabled education empowering every child in Rautahat to excel in higher studies and careers.
