E-Pustakalaya — Digital Library

E-Pustakalaya is Nepal's premier free digital library, providing access to thousands of educational books, school textbooks, literature and learning materials in Nepali and English. Available free for all students!

Why Use It

What E-Pustakalaya Offers

📗

Free School Textbooks

Access all CDC (Curriculum Development Centre) approved textbooks for Class 1 to 12 completely free.

📱

Read on Any Device

Works on mobile phones, tablets and computers. No installation needed — just open and read.

🇳🇵

Nepali & English Books

Thousands of books in Nepali and English covering all subjects — Science, Maths, Literature and more.

⬇️

Download for Offline Use

Download books as PDFs and read them offline — perfect for areas with limited internet connectivity.
