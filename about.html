Our Story

Empowering the Youth of Rautahat

, Bishrampur is a government-run institution located in the serene setting of Brindaban-02, Rautahat, Madhesh Province of Nepal. The school is dedicated to providing holistic, quality education to the children of our community.

From the nurturing environment of Early Childhood Development (ECD) to the rigorous academic programs of Class 11 and 12, the school provides a complete educational journey. Our state-of-the-art Computer Lab and Smart Classroom equip students with the skills they need to thrive in a digital world.

  • School TypeGovernment School
  • ClassesECD to Class 12
  • ProvinceMadhesh Province, Nepal
  • MunicipalityBrindaban-02, Rautahat
  • AffiliationNEB (Class 11–12), SEE Board (Class 10)
Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Values

Guiding principles that shape everything we do

🌟

Our Vision

To be a leading center of academic excellence in Rautahat, producing well-rounded, responsible citizens who contribute positively to Nepal and the world.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver inclusive, quality education using modern teaching methods, nurturing every student's intellectual, social, and moral development from ECD to Class 12.

💎

Our Values

Integrity, Respect, Inclusiveness, Excellence and Community Service. We believe education is the most powerful tool for transforming lives and society.
