Class Structure

From ECD to Class 12

Foundation Level
🌱 ECD — Early Childhood Development

A nurturing environment for young learners. Play-based learning, cognitive development, and social skills are the focus at this stage.

Primary Level
📚 Class 1 – 5

Building core foundations in Nepali, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies and more.

Lower Secondary
📖 Class 6 – 8

Deepening knowledge across subjects with introduction to computer science and additional languages.

Secondary Level — SEE Board
🎓 Class 9 – 10 (SEE)

Secondary Education Examination (SEE) level. Students prepare for board exams with dedicated faculty support and smart classroom resources.

Higher Secondary — NEB Affiliated
🏆 Class 11 – 12 (+2 Level)

Three specialized streams offered: Management, Education and Humanities. Affiliated with the National Examination Board (NEB).

+2 Programs

Class 11–12 Streams

📊

Management Stream

Class 11–12

Designed for future business leaders, entrepreneurs and professionals. Students gain expertise in financial and managerial concepts.

🎓

Education Stream

Class 11–12

For students aspiring to become teachers, education administrators or researchers. Focuses on pedagogy and child development.

📜

Humanities Stream

Class 11–12

Cultivating critical thinkers, writers and social scientists. Covers history, geography, sociology and literature.

Infrastructure

Our Facilities

Modern resources to support 21st-century learning

🖥️

Computer Laboratory

A fully equipped computer lab providing hands-on digital education. Students learn computer basics, typing, internet usage, and fundamental programming. Available for all classes.

📺

Smart Classroom

A cutting-edge smart room equipped with a large touch-screen interactive board, projector, and full audio-visual system. Makes complex concepts visual and engaging.

🏃

School Ground

A spacious school ground for sports, physical education, morning assembly and extracurricular activities. Promotes physical fitness and teamwork.

🌿

Healthy Environment

A peaceful, green campus environment that supports focused learning. Clean classrooms, good ventilation and a calm atmosphere contribute to better student outcomes.

Providing quality education from ECD to Class 12 in the heart of Rautahat, Madhesh Province, Nepal.
