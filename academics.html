🌱 Early Childhood Development (ECD)

Our ECD program provides a nurturing, play-based learning environment to prepare young children for primary schooling. Focuses on language development, motor skills, creativity, and social skills.

📐 Grade 1–10 (Basic & Secondary)

Following the CDC Nepal curriculum, our Basic and Secondary levels offer Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Nepali, English, ICT, and Health & Physical Education — culminating in the SEE examinations.
