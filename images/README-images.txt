Place your school photos in this folder with EXACTLY these filenames:

Required files:
  logo.png            ← School logo (square, PNG with transparent bg preferred)
  school-bg.jpg       ← Main school building / exterior photo (wide, landscape)
  principal.jpg       ← Principal Premlal Prasad Raut photo (square or portrait)
  computer-lab.jpg    ← Computer lab photo
  smart-room.jpg      ← Smart classroom / interactive board photo
  staff-room.jpg      ← Staff room photo
  assembly.jpg        ← School assembly / morning prayer photo
  ground.jpg          ← School ground / field photo
  students.jpg        ← Students studying in class photo

For committee members, create a subfolder:
  committee/
    adhyaksha.jpg     ← Adhyaksha (Chairman) photo
    member1.jpg       ← Member 1 photo
    member2.jpg       ← Member 2 photo
    (etc.)

After adding photos, push to GitHub and Vercel will deploy automatically.

Recommended image specs:
  - Format: .jpg or .png
  - Size: Keep under 2MB each (compress if needed)
  - Recommended tool: https://squoosh.app (free online image compressor)
