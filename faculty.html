Message from the Principal

Respected Parents, Guardians, and Dear Students,

It is with immense pride and humility that I welcome you to Shree Nepal Secondary School, Bishrampur. As the Principal of this esteemed institution, I consider it my greatest honor to lead a school that has been serving the educational needs of our community for years.

Education is not merely about acquiring knowledge from textbooks — it is about shaping character, developing critical thinking, and preparing our students to become responsible, compassionate, and capable citizens. At our school, we strive to create an environment where every child feels valued, safe, and inspired to achieve their personal best.

We are proud to offer a complete educational journey — from our nurturing Early Childhood Development (ECD) program all the way through to Class 12 with three streams: Management, Education, and Humanities. Our state-of-the-art Computer Laboratory and Smart Classroom ensure that our students are equipped with the digital skills demanded by the 21st century.

I extend my heartfelt gratitude to our dedicated teaching staff, the School Management Committee, parents, and the entire community for their unwavering support. Together, we are building a brighter future for the children of Rautahat.

I warmly invite all families to partner with us in this mission of nurturing excellence. Together, let us build a foundation of knowledge and values for our children that will serve them throughout their lives.

— Premlal Prasad Raut

Principal, Shree Nepal Secondary School Bishrampur
